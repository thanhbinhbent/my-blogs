This lesson covers PVs and PVCs:

- PersistentVolume (PV): pre-provisioned or dynamically created storage resource

- Reclaim policies: Retain, Delete, Recycle
- PV/PVC binding lifecycle
- Volume binding mode: Immediate vs WaitForFirstConsumer
